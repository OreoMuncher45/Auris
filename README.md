<h1><a id="features"></a>Features</h1>

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">

#### Flow — your endless mix
- **Personal soundtrack** combining favorites, history, skips and fresh finds. Unlocks at 16 favorites.
- **Temporary playlist** that grows as you listen; save it with your own name.
- **Moods** (Love, Workout, Chill, Sad, Focus, Party) and a **Flow Tuner** (discovery slider, genre switches) — edits batch until you hit Apply.
- **Apple-style page**: gradient hero, palette tint, Play/Shuffle/Save/Tune row.

#### Lossless, zero setup
- **Tidal Hi-Fi source, on by default** — genuine FLAC up to 24-bit/192kHz, no account, no keys.
- **Community instances with auto-failover** — the pool refreshes daily, dead ones drop silently, your custom URL always wins.
- **Fake-guard** — files wearing FLAC names but carrying lossy bitrates are refused automatically.
- **Honest stats** — lossless prints its PCM rate (1411), never a quiet passage's momentary dip. Same figure on Discord presence.

#### Playback
- **Search, browse and play** anything available on YouTube Music.
- **Gapless playback with true crossfade**, adjustable 0–12s.
- **Automix [Beta]** — DJ-style transitions with beat-matching and tempo-stretching.
- **Offline downloads** — save tracks with embedded metadata.
- **Local music library** integration.
- **Background playback** via a proper foreground media session.
- **Save cover art** at full quality from any song menu.

    </td>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">

#### Experience
- **Animated album canvas** — motion artwork on the now-playing screen.
- **Word-synced lyrics** — word/syllable-level highlighting from multiple sources.
- **Dynamic, artwork-driven theming** — Material palette extracted from album art.
- **Liquid Glass nav bar** (Android 12+, toggleable) plus frosted-glass UI.

#### Connectivity & Accounts
- **Sign in with your Google account** for personalized content.
- **Spotify integration** — connect your account to play your playlists and Liked Songs.
- **Discord Rich Presence** — live track, true quality line, and a Visit BitChord Next button.
- **Scrobbling** to Last.fm and ListenBrainz.
- **Pluggable sources** — add, edit, test and health-check addon servers.

#### Controls & Tweaks
- **Per-network audio quality** — separate quality ceilings for Wi-Fi and mobile data.
- **Playback speed control** (0.5×–2.0×) and **skip silence**.
- **Sleep timer** — fixed presets or "stop after this track".
- **System equalizer** integration.
- **Stats for nerds** — codec, bit depth, sample rate, and more on the now-playing screen.

    </td>

<h1><a id="download"></a>Download</h1>

Grab the latest APK from the [Releases](https://github.com/OreoMuncher45/BitChord/releases) page — pick the **arm64** build for most phones (universal fits all devices but is much larger). Sideloading requires enabling "Install unknown apps" for whichever app you download it with.

Builds are minified release builds, signed with the fork's stable key: install v1.5+ over older Next releases without uninstalling. (Upgrading from the very first Next builds needs one fresh install — the signing key changed once.)

For the Windows and Linux desktop app, see [DESKTOP.md](DESKTOP.md).
